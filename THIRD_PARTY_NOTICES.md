## Distribution boundary

The source export excludes node_modules, dist and generated bundles. Future binary/bundle distributions require a component-specific license and notice review. The license collection preserves selected upstream texts with clear retention value; it is not a complete binary distribution clearance. Preserve package-specific copyright notices, not merely a generic MIT text.

See docs/DEPENDENCY_RIGHTS_INVENTORY.json. Of 82 historical top-level text gaps, esrecurse has BSD text in its source header and imurmurhash has MIT text in README. Remaining evidence includes 52 esbuild platform entries, 25 Rollup platform entries, one lzma platform entry, @humanfs/types and natural-compare. Upstream project licenses and package declarations support these records but version/artifact-specific evidence remains incomplete. Missing local text is not proof of noncompliance.

TypeScript third-party notices, tslib copyright text, and Vite/Rollup bundled-component notices are preserved in licenses/third-party. Those notices are not replaced by FORM's MIT license.
